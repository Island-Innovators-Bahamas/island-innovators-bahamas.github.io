Student Interest Roster

Work With Island Innovators Bahamas

We are building a roster of students across STEM and the arts who want to contribute their skills to youth programming in The Bahamas.

Island Innovators Bahamas is a youth initiative that facilitates STEAM programs, workshops, and community events for young people in Nassau. Over two years we have run three cohorts across coding, robotics, music performance, and music production, most recently a two-week intensive at R.M. Bailey Senior High School that ended with a public student showcase.

We are now expanding to year-round programming, and we need people to build it with.

Where You Could Contribute

·Virtual or recorded workshops and masterclasses
·Filmed music sessions and interviews
·Games, challenges, and interactive activities
·Curriculum, research, and program development
·Graphic, creative, and program design
·Special projects and events

What You Gain

·Real experience applying your discipline to work that reaches students
·Named credit for substantial contributions to completed projects, programs, or events
·Leadership opportunities, including serving as a workshop or masterclass guest
·Access to a network across STEM and the arts built around serving young people

Before You Sign Up

This is an interest roster, not a job posting. Nothing here is paid, and signing up commits you to nothing. We reach out as specific opportunities come up.

Work is flexible and remote friendly, ranging from one-time projects to a few hours a week. Some opportunities may eventually involve travel to a program site.
